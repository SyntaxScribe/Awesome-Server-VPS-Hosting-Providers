# Contributing to Awesome Server / VPS Hosting Providers

Thanks for your interest in contributing! We follow the [Awesome Manifesto](https://awesome.re/manifesto) and keep this list **curated** — quality over quantity.

## How to Propose an Addition

1. **Use it or trust it** — Only propose providers you use and recommend, or that have strong, repeated endorsements from credible developer communities.
2. **Pick the right category** — Bare Metal, Cloud, VPS, Managed, or a Niche/Regional section.
3. **Add one concise bullet** —
   - **Name** (linked to official site) — *1 short, specific reason why it’s awesome.*
   - End the description with a period.
4. **Alphabetical order** within a section.
5. **No affiliate links**, coupons, or tracking params.
6. Open a **Pull Request** with a brief note on your experience/evidence.

## Style Guide

- Keep entries **concise** (ideally one line).
- Prefer **objective** reasons: performance, reliability, support quality, price/perf, unique features, locations.
- Avoid marketing superlatives unless backed by measurable facts.
- Use **American English** spelling for consistency.

## Code of Conduct

Be respectful and constructive. Discuss larger changes in an issue first.
