## Bare Metal (Dedicated)

- **[OVHcloud](https://www.ovhcloud.com)** – Budget-friendly, global network, anti-DDoS included, huge catalog.
- **[Hetzner Dedicated](https://www.hetzner.com/dedicated-rootserver)** – Exceptional value/performance (EU/USA); Server Auction for deals.
- **[Hivelocity](https://www.hivelocity.net)** – Premium dedicated; strong hardware options and support; many locations.
- **[Leaseweb](https://www.leaseweb.com)** – Global backbone; from budget to enterprise configs; good peering.
- **[Scaleway Dedibox](https://www.scaleway.com/en/dedicated-servers/)** – Solid EU dedicated; interesting line-up incl. ARM history.
- **[Servers.com](https://www.servers.com)** – Performance-oriented dedicated; private networking; multiple regions.
- **[PhoenixNAP Bare Metal Cloud](https://phoenixnap.com/bare-metal-cloud)** – API-driven bare metal; good support; US/EU.
- **[Vultr Bare Metal](https://www.vultr.com/bare-metal/)** – Hourly/monthly bare metal with simple UI and many POPs.
- **[Cherry Servers](https://www.cherryservers.com)** – Lithuania-based; fast turn-ups; dev-friendly automation.
- **[DataPacket](https://www.datapacket.com)** – High-bandwidth dedicated; 10–100 Gbps options; low-latency focus.
- **[Path.net Bare Metal](https://path.net)** – DDoS-hardened anycast network with bare-metal options for critical workloads.
- **[Zenlayer Bare Metal](https://www.zenlayer.com/bare-metal-cloud/)** – Strong in Asia/edge; on-demand bare metal worldwide.
- **[IBM Cloud Bare Metal](https://www.ibm.com/cloud/bare-metal)** – Enterprise-grade options incl. IBM Power; compliance.
- **[IONOS Dedicated](https://www.ionos.com/servers/dedicated)** – Europe/US; competitive entry pricing; good for SMBs.
- **[SoYouStart](https://www.soyoustart.com)** – OVH’s budget dedicated line; great for labs/hobby workloads.
- **[Kimsufi](https://www.kimsufi.com)** – Ultra-budget dedicated (OVH family); low-end but unbeatable price for dev toys.
- **[Latitude.sh (ex‑Maxihost)](https://www.latitude.sh)** – Brazil-born bare metal; excellent in LATAM + expanding global.
- **[ReliableSite](https://www.reliablesite.net)** – US dedicated with unmetered options; gamer/dev community favorite.
- **[OneProvider](https://oneprovider.com)** – Aggregate access to dedicated in many cities; wide location choice.
- **[Worldstream](https://www.worldstream.com)** – NL dedicated with strong network and unmetered options.
- **[Snel.com](https://www.snel.com)** – Netherlands; quick delivery dedicated; fair pricing.
- **[GTHost](https://gthost.com)** – Instant dedicated servers in many locations; hourly/monthly.
- **[OVH GAME](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en/bare-metal/game/)** – Game-optimized dedicated with special anti-DDoS profiles.
- **[1Gbits Dedicated](https://1gbits.com/buy-dedicated-server/)** – Global dedicated servers with rapid provisioning, unmetered bandwidth options, and many OS templates; crypto-friendly billing.
- **[MonoVM Dedicated](https://monovm.com/dedicated-server/)** – Worldwide dedicated servers with instant setup options, solid network, and flexible configurations; business- and developer-friendly pricing.

## Cloud IaaS & Developer Clouds

- **[Amazon Web Services (AWS)](https://aws.amazon.com)** – Market leader; EC2, managed services; massive global presence.
- **[Microsoft Azure](https://azure.microsoft.com)** – Enterprise integration; Windows/.NET first-class; many regions.
- **[Google Cloud (GCP)](https://cloud.google.com)** – Top-tier networking, Kubernetes/AI strengths; developer-friendly.
- **[Oracle Cloud (OCI)](https://www.oracle.com/cloud/)** – Generous free tier; strong price/perf; solid ARM (Ampere).
- **[IBM Cloud](https://www.ibm.com/cloud)** – Enterprise workloads, compliance, Power systems, hybrid via OpenShift.
- **[Alibaba Cloud](https://www.alibabacloud.com)** – Leader in China/APAC; ECS for compute; strong big-data stack.
- **[Tencent Cloud](https://www.tencentcloud.com)** – Competitive in China/APAC; media/gaming strengths.
- **[Huawei Cloud](https://www.huaweicloud.com/intl/en-us/)** – Growing global footprint; cost-effective instances.
- **[DigitalOcean](https://www.digitalocean.com)** – Beloved by devs; simple “Droplets”, docs, community.
- **[Linode (Akamai Cloud)](https://www.linode.com)** – Consistent performance; human support; transparent pricing.
- **[Vultr Cloud Compute](https://www.vultr.com)** – Many regions; simple UI/API; good entry pricing.
- **[Hetzner Cloud](https://www.hetzner.com/cloud)** – EU-centric; unbeatable value; shared/dedicated vCPU flavors.
- **[Scaleway Elements](https://www.scaleway.com/en/)** – EU cloud; K8s, object storage, fun instance types.
- **[UpCloud](https://upcloud.com)** – MaxIOPS storage; performance focus; EU/US/APAC.
- **[Exoscale](https://www.exoscale.com)** – Swiss/EU cloud; data sovereignty; great for EU compliance.
- **[OVHcloud Public Cloud](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en/public-cloud/)** – Flexible instances; good bandwidth pricing.
- **[Kamatera](https://www.kamatera.com)** – Very granular instance sizing; minute billing; many OS templates.
- **[CloudSigma](https://www.cloudsigma.com)** – Custom CPU/RAM mix; many regional DCs; dev-friendly SLAs.
- **[DreamHost DreamCompute](https://www.dreamhost.com/cloud/)** – OpenStack-based; simple pricing; US-based.
- **[Fly.io](https://fly.io)** – App-focused VMs close to users; great for globally distributed apps.
- **[France Nuage](https://france-nuage.fr)** – Sovereign open-source cloud hosted 100% in France; managed open-source apps and S3 storage.
- **[Render](https://render.com)** – PaaS-style but with persistent VMs; good developer UX.
- **[Scaleway Apple Silicon](https://www.scaleway.com/en/mac-mini-m1/)** – Managed M1 minis for CI/builds.

## VPS (Unmanaged)

- **[Contabo](https://contabo.com)** – Ultra-competitive specs per $; great for labs/storage; EU/US DCs.
- **[Netcup](https://www.netcup.eu)** – German “root servers” at sharp prices; frequent promos; solid performance.
- **[BuyVM (Frantech)](https://buyvm.net)** – Community favorite value VPS; add-ons (Block Storage, DirectAdmin).
- **[RackNerd](https://racknerd.com)** – Popular in LowEnd community; frequent deals; many US locations.
- **[RamNode](https://www.ramnode.com)** – Long-trusted SSD VPS; low-latency; no-nonsense reliability.
- **[Time4VPS](https://www.time4vps.com)** – Storage VPS steals; Lithuania DC; great for backups.
- **[HostHatch](https://hosthatch.com)** – Strong storage/VDS offerings; good network; popular with power users.
- **[MivoCloud](https://www.mivocloud.com)** – Moldova-based; affordable; decent EU latency; simple panel.
- **[LunaNode](https://www.lunanode.com)** – Canada; hourly billing; feature-rich (load balancers, snapshots).
- **[Webdock](https://webdock.io)** – Denmark; developer-friendly VPS; green energy focus; great UI.
- **[Aruba Cloud Smart VPS](https://www.arubacloud.com/virtual-private-server/)** – Italy/EU; rock-bottom intro pricing.
- **[OVHcloud VPS](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en/vps/)** – Affordable, anti-DDoS included; good bandwidth.
- **[Hetzner CX/CPX](https://www.hetzner.com/cloud)** – Legendary value; excellent for EU workloads; API + CLI.
- **[Linode](https://www.linode.com)** – Balanced price/perf; 11 global regions; helpful docs/community.
- **[DigitalOcean Droplets](https://www.digitalocean.com/products/droplets/)** – Clean UX, predictable pricing, one-click apps.
- **[Vultr High Frequency/Regular](https://www.vultr.com/products/cloud-compute/)** – Region variety; HF for snappy CPU.
- **[UpCloud](https://upcloud.com/products/cloud-servers/)** – Great I/O; premium feel; reserved/ephemeral options.
- **[Scaleway Instances](https://www.scaleway.com/en/virtual-instances/)** – EU options incl. ARM; good for side-projects.
- **[Lightsail (AWS)](https://aws.amazon.com/lightsail/)** – Simplified VPS on AWS infra; flat pricing; snapshots, DNS.
- **[A2 Hosting Unmanaged](https://www.a2hosting.com/vps-hosting/unmanaged)** – US-centric; turbo options; dev-friendly.
- **[Hostwinds Unmanaged](https://www.hostwinds.com/vps/unmanaged)** – Flexible plans; steady performance; easy upgrades.
- **[InterServer VPS](https://www.interserver.net/vps/)** – Long-standing US provider; lock-in pricing; many templates.
- **[Clouding.io](https://clouding.io)** – Spain-based; hourly billing; simple panel; good for Iberia/EU.
- **[GreenCloudVPS](https://greencloudvps.com)** – Budget-friendly KVM/VDS; many locations; frequent promos.
- **[VPSDime](https://vpsdime.com)** – High-RAM deals at low prices; good for caches/dev boxes.
- **[Scaleforce/Kamatera VPS](https://www.kamatera.com)** – Minute billing; scale up/down precisely.
- **[Feral Hosting](https://www.feralhosting.com)** – Niche dev community favorite; seedbox-oriented Linux boxes.
- **[1Gbits VPS](https://1gbits.com/buy-vps-hosting/)** – Global Linux/Windows VPS with fast activation and many locations; fair pricing and crypto payments available.
- **[MonoVM VPS](https://monovm.com/vps-server/)** – KVM-based Linux/Windows VPS and RDP; worldwide locations; flexible billing including crypto.

## Managed VPS & “Platform” VPS

- **[KnownHost](https://www.knownhost.com)** – Best-in-class managed VPS; proactive support; business-friendly.
- **[Liquid Web](https://www.liquidweb.com)** – Premium managed VPS/dedicated; “Heroic Support”; high performance.
- **[Cloudways](https://www.cloudways.com)** – Orchestrates DO/Vultr/Linode/AWS/GCP for you; great for PHP/WordPress apps.
- **[A2 Hosting Managed VPS](https://www.a2hosting.com/vps-hosting/managed)** – Managed stack, caching, staging; helpful support.
- **[InMotion Managed VPS](https://www.inmotionhosting.com/vps-hosting)** – US provider; reliable managed cPanel stacks.
- **[ScalaHosting (SPanel)](https://www.scalahosting.com)** – Managed VPS with SPanel (cPanel alternative); good value.
- **[RoseHosting](https://www.rosehosting.com)** – Fully managed Linux VPS; will install/customize on request.
- **[Nexcess (by Liquid Web)](https://www.nexcess.net)** – Managed WooCommerce/Magento/WordPress on VPS-like infra.
- **[RunCloud / Ploi / Forge](https://runcloud.io)** – Not providers, but beloved server managers for DIY on your own VPS/cloud.

## Niche / Specialty Providers

### GPU Cloud
- **[Lambda Cloud](https://lambdalabs.com/cloud)** – NVIDIA GPUs for AI training; easy Jupyter workflows.
- **[CoreWeave](https://www.coreweave.com)** – Specialized large-scale GPU cloud; fast interconnect; popular for AI/VFX.
- **[RunPod](https://www.runpod.io)** – Developer-friendly GPU instances; serverless pods; community templates.
- **[Paperspace (by DigitalOcean)](https://www.digitalocean.com/products/paperspace/)** – GPUs with simple UX; notebooks; integrations.
- **[Vast.ai](https://vast.ai)** – GPU marketplace; great for cost hunters; many card types/regions.
- **[Crusoe Cloud](https://www.crusoecloud.com)** – GPU compute with sustainability angle (flare gas mitigations).
- **[NVIDIA RTX/AI via major clouds]** – AWS/GCP/Azure/OCI provide managed GPU families at scale.

### Storage-Heavy VPS
- **[Hetzner Storage (CX/CPX + Storage Boxes)](https://www.hetzner.com)** – Affordable big disks; snapshots; Storage Boxes (SFTP/WebDAV/Borg).
- **[HostHatch Storage Plans](https://hosthatch.com)** – Big-disk KVM deals; frequent promos; good for backups.
- **[Time4VPS Storage](https://www.time4vps.com/storage-vps/)** – Very low price/TB; EU DC; simple panel.
- **[Contabo Storage](https://contabo.com)** – High disk allocations; competitive bandwidth.
- **[rsync.net](https://www.rsync.net)** – Not VPS but beloved offsite storage (ZFS, snapshots, rsync/Borg).

### Game & DDoS-Focused
- **[OVH GAME Dedicated](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en/bare-metal/game/)** – Game-tuned anti-DDoS; gamer communities love it.
- **[Path.net](https://path.net)** – Anycast DDoS mitigation + bare metal/PoPs; critical workloads.
- **[Psychz Networks](https://www.psychz.net)** – DDoS-protected dedicated/VPS; peering in gamer hubs.
- **[Voxility (via partners)](https://www.voxility.com)** – Big DDoS capacity; widely used via resellers/providers.

### Privacy / Offshore
- **[1984 Hosting](https://www.1984hosting.com)** – Iceland-based; privacy ethos; VPS/dedicated.
- **[OrangeWebsite](https://www.orangewebsite.com)** – Iceland; free-speech friendly; offshore hosting.
- **[FlokiNET](https://flokinet.is)** – Iceland/Romania/Finland; privacy-minded VPS/dedicated.
- **[BuyVM LU](https://buyvm.net)** – Luxembourg location option; friendly to privacy-conscious users.

### ARM / Alternative Architectures
- **[Oracle Cloud ARM (Ampere)](https://www.oracle.com/cloud/compute/arm/)** – Generous always-free ARM; great perf/$.
- **[AWS Graviton](https://aws.amazon.com/ec2/graviton/)** – ARM instances with excellent perf/watt; wide service support.
- **[Scaleway ARM Instances](https://www.scaleway.com/en/virtual-instances/arm/)** – Long ARM history; fun for devs and labs.

## Contributing

Contributions are welcome! Please ensure any suggested provider is **truly awesome**:

- You have used it and would personally recommend it, **or**
- It has strong, consistent endorsements from trusted developer communities.

**Guidelines**
- Add one provider per bullet with: **Name** – short, specific reason why it’s awesome (value, performance, support, unique features).
- Keep descriptions concise and end with a period.
- Group in the most appropriate category and keep alphabetical order within groups where possible.
- No affiliate links. Official websites only.
- Open a PR and briefly explain your recommendation and experience.

See also the [Awesome contributing template](https://github.com/sindresorhus/awesome/blob/main/contributing.md).
